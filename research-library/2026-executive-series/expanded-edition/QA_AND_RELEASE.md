## Evidence boundaries

This series is independent desk research and original modelling, not audited case-study outcomes or third-party peer-reviewed publications. All calculated monetary examples are hypothetical. The Asian leadership paper avoids cultural generalisations and does not claim original executive interviews. Historical interpretations are presented as interpretative, not causal evidence. The AI paper distinguishes occupational exposure from job losses. Current policy/legal requirements and issuer claims need relevant independent review before being applied to a particular organisation or jurisdiction.
