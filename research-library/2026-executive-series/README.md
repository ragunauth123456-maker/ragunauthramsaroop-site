# Four executive white papers | September 2026

Independent research by Ragunauth Ramsaroop. Four LinkedIn posts about McKinsey publications prompted four distinct research questions. These white papers do not reproduce McKinsey's text, illustrations or visual identity and are not affiliated with McKinsey.

## Editorial summaries

1. [Where Capital Meets Capability](where-capital-meets-capability/README.md): Why increased technology funding is not equivalent to proven industrial capability. Introduces the Capital-to-Capability Gate.
2. [From Climate Data to Cash Flow](from-climate-data-to-cash-flow/README.md): A measurement and governance method linking physical climate information to auditable financial outcomes. Introduces the Four-Ledger Climate Value Model.
3. [The Architecture of Asian Leadership](architecture-of-asian-leadership/README.md): Examines classical ideas, founder succession, governance and organisational adaptation without attributing uniform values to diverse Asian business cultures.
4. [Trust Is Infrastructure](trust-is-infrastructure/README.md): A practical model connecting human agency, model evaluation, redress, work design and measurable outcomes. Introduces the Five Trust Contracts.

## Publication package

Four complete 2026 executive manuscripts were prepared independently, each containing an executive briefing, original analytical model, scenarios or applications, limitations, appendix and references. Each is accompanied by an 11–12-page PDF and a separate six-page LinkedIn-style visual briefing. The complete local PDF, manuscript and carousel files have been delivered to the author as a downloadable archive.

**Repository scope:** This pull request contains editorial overviews and source references. It does **not** include the final PDF or full manuscript binaries. Those assets must be uploaded separately before any public-facing website index should claim they are hosted on GitHub.

## Editorial principles

- Cite dated primary research where available. Mark extrapolations and forecasts.
- Identify hypothetical scenarios and avoid invented client or interview data.
- Disclose methodological limits, counterfactuals, sensitive claim boundaries and unverified outcomes.
- Distinguish executive judgment questions from source claims and from actual causal findings.
