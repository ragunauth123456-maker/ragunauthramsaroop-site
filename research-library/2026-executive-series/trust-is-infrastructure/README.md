# Trust Is Infrastructure

A board-level framework for human-centred AI transformation and accountable decision systems

Ragunauth Ramsaroop | Independent white paper | September 2026

> GitHub editorial overview. The complete research manuscript and publication PDF form separate deliverables. This overview does not pretend to be the full manuscript. The paper was independently written after viewing a McKinsey social media post; no affiliation or endorsement is implied.

AI adoption depends on technical performance, workflow integration, legal and safety controls, and credible relationships with people affected by the system. Trust is one required condition in many consequential workflows, not a substitute for financial viability or verified model quality. This independent paper connects human agency to an auditable 180-day deployment protocol.

## Original contribution: The Five Trust Contracts

**Purpose** establishes lawful boundaries and proportionality. **Evidence** requires representative evaluation and independent proof of performance. **Agency** protects informed worker judgment and task-level transition. **Redress** gives affected people a usable route to challenge consequential outputs. **Shared gains** makes productivity measurement and the allocation of benefits transparent.

## Issues examined

- The difference between AI access, genuine workflow adoption and fully costed productivity improvement.
- The ILO's occupational-exposure findings and their limits: exposure is not demonstrated job loss.
- NIST's Govern, Map, Measure and Manage functions as a governance structure.
- Evaluation design, severe-error thresholds, privacy, worker consultation, appeals and rollback.
- Four operating environments: finance, industry, public/health services and small or emerging-market enterprises.

## Evidence and use

The full manuscript includes an illustrative 100-worker cost sensitivity example, a 180-day operational sequence, enterprise risk scenarios, a seven-indicator board dashboard and appendices for use-case charters and independent review. Example financial results are hypothetical.
