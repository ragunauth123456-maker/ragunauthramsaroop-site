# The Architecture of Asian Leadership

Classical ideas, contemporary enterprise governance and durable institutions

Ragunauth Ramsaroop | Independent white paper | September 2026

> GitHub editorial overview. The complete research manuscript and publication PDF form separate deliverables. This overview does not pretend to be the full manuscript. The paper was independently written after viewing a McKinsey social media post; no affiliation or endorsement is implied.

Asian business settings include diverse ownership structures, legal systems, workforce patterns and competitive environments. No single national or philosophical tradition explains the conduct of individual executives. This independent paper draws critical, historically contextual questions from Kautilya's Arthashastra, Confucian thought and the contested Shu-Ha-Ri learning analogy, then tests modern corporate challenges through observable institutions rather than cultural stereotypes.

## Original contribution: Continuity, Legitimacy and Adaptation

**Continuity** concerns succession, treasury discipline, documentation and the durability of customer and supplier relationships. **Legitimacy** concerns independent oversight, conflict disclosure, worker voice and fair treatment of minority interests. **Adaptation** concerns documented learning, controlled experimentation, reliable performance measurement and the ability to retire unsuccessful initiatives.

## Issues examined

- How premodern political thought can inform questions without becoming an excuse for surveillance or unaccountable authority.
- The difference between personal loyalty and institutional trust.
- Why Shu-Ha-Ri serves as a limited learning analogy rather than a proven universal business model. Historical origins and transfer to management remain contested.
- Succession stress tests for founder-led, family-controlled, widely held and state-influenced enterprises.
- A twelve-question governance evidence register, three illustrative enterprise scenarios and a practical board agenda.

## Evidence and use

The full manuscript includes original conceptual illustrations, research limitations, a board evidence template and a future interview protocol. No original CEO interviews or invented company case studies are claimed.
