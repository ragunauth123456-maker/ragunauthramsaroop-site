## Correction policy

A future correction is material if it changes:
- a legal status;
- a material number;
- a source attribution;
- a chronology;
- a fiscal calculation;
- an environmental or social outcome characterization;
- an audit conclusion;
- a publication-status claim.

Material corrections should receive a new register entry, date, edition reference and disposition. Silent alteration of a material claim is not permitted.
