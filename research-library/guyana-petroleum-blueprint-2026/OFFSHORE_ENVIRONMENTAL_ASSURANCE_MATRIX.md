# OFFSHORE ENVIRONMENTAL ASSURANCE MATRIX
## Project-by-project control

Edition 2.0 treats environmental governance as project-specific. A permit is not evidence of compliance, and an EIA is not evidence of operational performance.

The machine-readable matrix separates five evidence layers:
1. legal/project authorization;
2. EIA and baseline documentation;
3. financial assurance;
4. spill-response obligations;
5. observed compliance/performance.

### Release rule

A development may be described as "authorized" when the permit/licence record establishes authorization.

It may be described as "compliant" only for a defined period and condition when regulator or independently verifiable compliance evidence supports that statement.

It may be described as "financially assured" only to the extent that the operative insurance, guarantee or other instruments have been reviewed. Permit wording alone establishes an obligation, not necessarily the amount, liquidity or future enforceability of the instrument.

### Highest-priority evidence gaps

- executed project-specific parent/affiliate guarantees;
- exact insurance limits, exclusions and renewal terms;
- permit-condition compliance schedules;
- flaring and emissions by project and event type;
- incident register;
- marine water/sediment/biological monitoring;
- spill drills and deployment-time evidence;
- decommissioning cost/security updates.

The companion dataset is `OFFSHORE_ENVIRONMENTAL_ASSURANCE_MATRIX.csv`.
