## Legal-language rules for Edition 1.2

1. Use "published in the Official Gazette" when the evidence establishes gazetting.
2. Use "commenced" only where a commencement provision or order establishes operation.
3. Use "the government states", "EPA states", or "contemporaneous reporting states" where authenticated judicial reasons are unavailable.
4. Never equate unlimited liability with an unlimited financial-security instrument unless the controlling judgment or instrument says so.
5. Never describe a negotiated or announced guarantee amount as equivalent to reviewed, enforceable and liquid financial protection without the executed instrument.
6. Separate permit wording, permit compliance, insurer strength, guarantee amount and loss-event recoverability.
7. Record appeal status by date because the legal position may change after the evidence cut-off.
8. Preserve paragraph and section pinpoints for every material legal proposition.
