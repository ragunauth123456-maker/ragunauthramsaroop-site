Flagship target: 520-560 substantive pages

## Publication rule

The flagship is not a longer version of the working paper. It is a reference work built from distinct evidence modules, original analytical exhibits, country comparisons, legal and fiscal crosswalks, environmental documentation, counterarguments, failure cases and an implementation handbook.

A page counts toward the target only when it contains substantive analysis, a documented exhibit, a source-audited case study, a reproducible model or a technical annex required to test the argument. Repeated boilerplate, blank leaves, oversized quotations and duplicated bibliographies do not count.

## Total planned substantive range

The architecture provides approximately 550 substantive pages before final typesetting adjustments. The acceptable final range is 520-560 substantive pages. Any lower page count must reflect editorial compression, not omission of required evidence. Any higher page count must arise from genuinely necessary analysis, appendices or source documentation, not padding.

## Mandatory analytical modules across all parts

Every relevant chapter must address:

- what happened;
- when it happened;
- which legal instrument controlled;
- which institution owned the decision;
- what evidence is independently verifiable;
- what government or operator states;
- what critics or affected groups state;
- what remains uncertain;
- what would falsify the chapter's central inference;
- what another country might adapt;
- what depends on Guyana-specific conditions.

## Source standards

Tier 1: executed agreements, acts, regulations, court decisions, permits, audited accounts, central-bank statements, regulator datasets and official registries.

Tier 2: IMF, World Bank, EITI, multilateral technical reports, official statistics and audited corporate disclosures.

Tier 3: peer-reviewed academic literature, credible independent research and established technical institutions.

Tier 4: documented stakeholder submissions, civil-society analysis, investigative reporting and industry commentary, clearly attributed and cross-checked.

No material claim should rely solely on Tier 4 where primary evidence exists.

4. NRF figures reconcile across published accounts.
5. Production capacity and realized production remain separate.
6. Environmental permits and project records are checked individually.
7. Social outcomes use appropriately dated evidence.
8. Comparative cases do not become political rankings.

10. Contradictions and unresolved questions remain visible.
11. Searchable PDF text, bookmarks, citations, tables and figures pass QA.
12. No external peer review is claimed unless independent reviewers complete one and their scope is recorded.
13. A SHA-256 publication manifest is generated.
14. The public website is updated only after the final files and QA evidence exist.
