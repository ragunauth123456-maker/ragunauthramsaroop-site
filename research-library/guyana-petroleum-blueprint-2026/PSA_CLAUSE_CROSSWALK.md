# STABROEK PSA CLAUSE CROSSWALK
## Executed 2016 agreement versus later legal/policy architecture

This crosswalk is a navigation and audit control. It is not a substitute for reading the executed agreement.

### Use rules

- The executed 2016 Stabroek agreement controls analysis of Stabroek contractual rights and obligations.
- The 2023 model PSA and Petroleum Activities Act 2023 are later frameworks and may not be substituted retroactively.
- A clause reference establishes where the mechanism is located. It does not settle a contested legal interpretation.
- Detailed legal conclusions should cite exact text, version and context.
- Fiscal modelling should connect Article 11, Article 13, Article 15 and Annex C rather than discussing them independently.

### Core analytical chain

**Production volume -> Article 13 valuation -> royalty -> Article 11 cost recovery -> Article 11 profit petroleum -> Article 15 tax treatment -> cash entitlement -> NRF deposit.**

Any model that skips one of those links risks mixing petroleum tax, cost recovery and sovereign cash accounting.

### Audit chain

**Annex C cost statement -> supporting invoice/allocation -> audit exception -> contractor response -> government determination -> dispute if any -> cost-bank adjustment -> eventual cash/revenue effect.**

An exception is not the same as cash recovered.
