# Publication Review Gates
## No flagship release until each gate is evidenced

### Gate 1: Chronology
- Exact dates for discovery, PSA signature, PSA effective date, production licences, NRF statutes, first oil and each project start.
- Earlier exploration and 2009 LCDS clearly separated from post-discovery institutions.
- Every event tagged as observed, enacted, commenced, announced, projected or disputed.

### Gate 2: Petroleum contract
- Executed 2016 Stabroek text checked directly.
- Simplified royalty/cost/profit examples reconciled to agreement language.
- 2023 model terms never applied retroactively.
- Audit, tax, valuation, decommissioning and dispute provisions traced.

### Gate 3: Production
- Monthly series through latest verified 2026 observation.
- Capacity, actual daily output, monthly output, lifts and receipts stored as separate fields.
- Startup dates and sanctioned projects independently dated.

### Gate 4: NRF
- Annual opening balance, receipts, investment income, transfers and closing balance reconciled.
- 2019, 2021 and 2024 legal versions crosswalked.
- Cash balances not mixed with forecasts or accrual estimates.
- Public investment outcomes kept separate from fund accounting.

### Gate 5: Environment
- Permit and EIA register per producing project.
- 2025 oil-pollution statute commencement status verified from authoritative instruments.
- Actual insurance, guarantee and liability documents reviewed where public.
- Court decisions read from authentic text before legal characterization.
- Spill-response preparedness assessed with disclosed evidence limits.

### Gate 6: Social and local value
- Local-content figures distinguish gross procurement, domestic ownership, wages and retained value.
- Household welfare uses current survey dates.
- No 2019 poverty measure described as a 2026 poverty rate.
- Amerindian and hinterland outcomes include independent or community-level evidence where available.

### Gate 7: Climate and natural capital
- Forest-carbon credits, contract values, issued units, receipts and village disbursements reconciled.
- Petroleum emissions and forest-carbon accounting kept separate.
- No claim of economy-wide carbon neutrality without full emissions accounting.
- Carbon-market methodology changes and eligibility status dated.

### Gate 8: EITI and governance
- 2026 Validation status checked on publication date.
- EITI Board concerns attributed accurately.
- Contract publication, beneficial ownership, cost audits and procurement disclosures assessed separately.
- No political ranking or judgment.

### Gate 9: Comparators
- Ghana, Norway, Timor-Leste and Suriname use current primary legal or fund sources.
- Every comparator includes transfer limits.
- No institutional ranking or selected political winner.

### Gate 10: Quantitative models
- All assumptions visible.
- Units tested.
- Sensitivities reproducible.
- Forecast ranges never described as expected fact.
- No confidential company data.

### Gate 11: Editorial integrity
- Zero fabricated citations.
- Each material number has a source and observation date.
- Contradictory evidence is preserved.
- External review described as pending until completed.
- Corrections register maintained.

### Gate 12: PDF QA
- Searchable text.
- Correct page numbering and bookmarks.
- Tables readable at normal zoom.
- Figures have alt text/captions and source notes.
- No filler or repeated pages.
- Actual substantive page count verified after rendering.
- SHA-256 checksum recorded.
- Website links tested before publication.

### Required external reviewers
Independent review should cover:
- petroleum fiscal economics;
- Guyana petroleum law and contract interpretation;
- public financial management;
- offshore environmental science and spill response;
- Indigenous/community safeguards;
- macroeconomics;
- data visualization and statistical interpretation.

External review remains a requirement, not a completed fact.
