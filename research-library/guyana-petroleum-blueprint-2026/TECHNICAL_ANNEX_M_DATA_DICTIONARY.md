# TECHNICAL ANNEX M
## Data dictionary, indicator definitions and reproducible publication tables

Purpose: ensure that major numbers in the flagship edition use stable definitions.

## M1. Petroleum production

Unit: barrels of crude oil.
Frequency: daily/monthly.
Primary source: regulator dashboard where available.
Do not mix with nameplate capacity.

## M2. Nameplate capacity

Unit: barrels per day.
Definition: engineering design throughput.
Not an observed annual average.

## M3. Government lift

Unit: cargo/barrels.
Definition: petroleum entitlement physically lifted for government account.

## M4. Profit-oil receipt

Unit: USD or reporting currency.
Definition: cash proceeds from government profit-oil sale settled in period.

## M5. Royalty

Unit: currency.
Definition: contractual royalty paid under the applicable agreement.

## M6. Petroleum deposit

Definition: qualifying petroleum cash deposited into the NRF during the period.

Do not include projected future sales.

## M7. Fund investment income

Definition: interest, dividend or other investment income under fund accounting.

Keep separate from petroleum receipts.

## M8. Fund market value

Definition: end-period fair value of financial assets.

May differ from cash.

## M9. Fund withdrawal

Definition: lawful transfer from NRF under the statutory framework.

Not equivalent to final public spending.

## M10. Consolidated-fund receipt

Definition: cash received by the government's budget account following withdrawal.

## M11. Capital appropriation

Definition: parliament-approved spending authority.

Not equivalent to contract award or completed asset.

## M12. Capital expenditure

Definition: amount recorded as spent under public accounts.

Must be distinguished from physical completion.

## M13. Project completion

Definition: physical works substantially complete according to defined criteria.

Separate from commissioning.

## M14. Commissioning

Definition: asset has passed tests and is available for intended service.

## M15. Service outcome

Definition: measured benefit such as travel-time reduction, power reliability or health service.

## M16. Gross local procurement

Definition: invoice value purchased from entities counted under local-content reporting.

Not domestic value added.

## M17. Domestic value added

Definition: value created domestically through wages, operating surplus and local taxes net of imported intermediate inputs.

## M18. Local employment

State whether definition is citizenship, residence or statutory local-content category.

## M19. Training participant

Person enrolled.

Separate from completion and employment.

## M20. Household poverty

Requires:
- survey year;
- poverty line;
- methodology;
- population coverage.

Never carry an old estimate forward as current.

## M21. Real household consumption

Household expenditure or consumption adjusted for prices.

Useful for distributional analysis.

## M22. Real GDP

Output volume measured at constant prices.

State base year.

## M23. Non-oil GDP

Define excluded petroleum activities and any treatment of petroleum-related services.

## M24. Non-oil primary balance

Fiscal balance excluding petroleum revenue and interest, under the adopted methodology.

State denominator.

## M25. Public debt

Report gross and, where useful, net of liquid financial assets.

## M26. Construction-cost index

Separate from CPI.

Tracks cost pressure in capital programme.

## M27. Flaring

Specify gas volume, CO2e or duration.

Do not mix units.

## M28. Spill

Specify substance, estimated volume, confidence and affected area.

## M29. Financial assurance

Specify instrument, amount, currency, trigger and expiry.

Do not confuse with unlimited legal liability.

## M30. Forest-credit issuance

Units issued under the applicable standard.

Separate from sale.

## M31. Carbon-credit contract value

Future contractual consideration subject to delivery.

Separate from cash received.

## M32. Carbon-credit cash receipt

Actual cash received in period.

## M33. Community allocation

Amount allocated or transferred under programme rules.

Separate from outcome.

## M34. EITI status

Use official EITI process status and date.

Do not invent a score while validation is ongoing.

## M35. Audit adjustment

Specify proposed, agreed or final.

Do not label proposed amount recovered.

## M36. Source table convention

Every table footer should include:
- source IDs;
- observation date;
- unit;
- calculation;
- rounding.

## M37. Forecast table convention

Forecast tables use:
- scenario name;
- assumptions;
- date prepared;
- uncertainty.

They should never appear visually identical to historical actuals.

## M38. Revision triangle

For key annual forecasts, preserve:
- original budget;
- mid-year revision;
- outturn;
- later audited outturn.

This shows forecast quality.

## M39. Geographic coding

Use Guyana's administrative regions for public-service data when available.

Offshore projects use block/project coordinates.

## M40. Currency

Identify:
- USD;
- GYD;
- exchange rate;
- nominal/real.

Do not compare multi-year nominal GYD without adjustment.

## M41. Rounding

Totals may differ because of rounding. Store unrounded calculations.

## M42. Missing data

Use blank/NA.

Do not substitute zero.

## M43. Estimated data

Mark with "estimate" and source.

## M44. Confidential data

State why unavailable and what public proxy is used.

## M45. Indicator provenance

Each chart should link to the source register.

## M46. Machine-readable supplement

The final publication package should include CSV tables for:
- production;
- fund;
- scenarios;
- comparator metrics;
- project register;
- source register.

## M47. Checksums

Publish SHA-256 for PDF and data supplement.

## M48. Reproducibility

A user should be able to recalculate displayed tables using the published data and formulas.

## M49. Archive date

The online research page should display the evidence cut-off and edition date.

## M50. Annex conclusion

Stable definitions protect a long publication from internal contradiction. The data dictionary should be treated as part of the research, not an afterthought.
