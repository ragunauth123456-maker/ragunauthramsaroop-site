# EDITION 2.0 CORE EXPANSION STANDARD
## Guyana's Petroleum Transformation

Edition 2.0 is a manuscript rebuild, not a page-count exercise.

### Substance rule

A section qualifies as core analysis only when it does at least one of the following:
1. establishes a dated factual sequence using registered evidence;
2. explains a legal, fiscal, environmental, social or institutional mechanism;
3. performs or explains a reproducible quantitative reconciliation;
4. compares mechanisms across countries using common dimensions;
5. identifies an adverse case, failure mode or counterfactual;
6. converts evidence into an operational decision rule for a newcomer state.

Repeated summaries, restated bullets, source lists and page-padding language do not count toward the core target.

### Evidence-density rule

Every major factual section should contain:
- a defined claim or question;
- source IDs from SOURCE_REGISTER.md;
- observation date or legal version where material;
- evidence class: primary, official secondary, multilateral, operator, independent or stakeholder;
- explicit limits where the evidence does not establish outcome or causality.

High-risk legal, fiscal, environmental and social propositions must also appear in CLAIM_SOURCE_MATRIX.md or a successor master evidence matrix.

### Quantitative minimum

Edition 2.0 must include reproducible structured datasets for at least:
- petroleum exploration and project chronology;
- offshore project register;
- production/startup sequence;
- NRF annual stock-flow ledger;
- public-investment conversion register;
- local-content domestic-value framework;
- environmental permit/financial-assurance register;
- comparator mechanism matrix;
- adverse scenarios.

### Publication architecture

The ten Parts are the book. Evidence dossiers and technical annexes support the book and may not be used to manufacture a page target.

Final audit must separately report:
- integrated-core words;
- integrated-core pages;
- annex/workbook words and pages;
- source-register pages;
- total rendered pages.

### Release discipline

Edition 2.0 remains a development manuscript until:
- integrated core exceeds 120,000 words;
- source and high-risk claim integrity checks pass;
- the quantitative datasets reconcile;
- visual PDF QA passes;
- unresolved legal/evidence items remain clearly labelled;
- external peer review is either completed or explicitly reported as pending.

No internal audit may be described as independent external peer review.
