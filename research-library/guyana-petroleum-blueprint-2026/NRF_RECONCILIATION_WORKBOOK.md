# NATURAL RESOURCE FUND RECONCILIATION WORKBOOK

Companion technical annex for Part IV of the flagship publication.  
Working status: partially populated from audited and official period reports.  
Evidence cut-off: 27 September 2026.

## Why this workbook exists

Natural Resource Fund figures appear in several valid but different forms:

- petroleum revenue recognized under financial reporting;
- cash received into the fund;
- revenue receivable at period end;
- investment income;
- government withdrawals;
- transfers to the Consolidated Fund;
- cash and cash equivalents;
- market value;
- closing net assets;
- annual forecasts.

The flagship must never place these values in one time series without reconciling their accounting basis.

## Control equation

For a cash-based period bridge:

Opening cash  
+ cash petroleum receipts  
+ cash investment receipts  
+ other cash inflows  
- transfers/withdrawals  
- cash expenses  
= closing cash.

For audited accrual statements, add or subtract receivables, payables, unrealized gains/losses and any classification changes required by the financial statements.

Audited statement of income, G$'000:
- Royalties: 2,694,223
- Profit oil: 38,651,798
- Interest on deposits: 1,581

- Total income: 41,348,595

Audited cash-flow statement, G$'000:
- Net cash flow from operating activities: 31,060,827
- Government withdrawal: 0
- Closing cash and cash equivalents: 31,060,827

The notes identify revenue receivables of G$10,287,768, representing the fourth 2020 profit-oil lift, which was due in January 2021.

The notes report three royalty cash deposits during 2020 totaling USD 12,921,933.23.

The audited profit-oil note recognizes four 2020 lifts totaling USD 185,380,324.44, while the fourth lift was a year-end receivable rather than 2020 cash.

This produces the first key reconciliation rule:

**2020 recognized petroleum revenue is not identical to 2020 cash petroleum deposits.**

## Cash-receipt chronology control

Bank of Guyana Table 6 supplies a dated petroleum cash-receipt history from inception through 30 June 2025.

The final reproducible dataset should store every transaction with:

- deposit date;
- petroleum source;
- FPSO/project where identified;
- receipt type: profit oil or royalty;
- USD amount;
- cargo/lift identifier where public;
- production period;
- sale/lift date;
- invoice date;
- receivable-recognition period;
- fund-deposit period.

The paper should then generate two separate annual charts:

1. **Cash deposited by calendar year.**
2. **Petroleum revenue recognized by accounting year.**

Differences between the charts are timing differences unless other accounting adjustments apply.

## Withdrawal-rule legal crosswalk to build

- actual transfer dates.

## Public-investment separation

The NRF workbook ends when money reaches the Consolidated Fund.

A separate public-investment dataset must follow funds into:
- appropriation;
- procurement;
- contract award;
- physical completion;
- commissioning;
- maintenance;
- service delivery;
- beneficiary outcomes.

This separation is required to avoid treating a lawful withdrawal as proof of a completed national-development result.

1. Extract audited 2022-2025 statements.

3. Reconcile every annual opening balance to prior closing balance.
4. Reconcile receivables across year boundaries.

6. Reconcile withdrawals to parliamentary notifications and Consolidated Fund records.
7. Record investment income separately.
8. Preserve all restatements.
9. Explain differences between market value, cash, net assets and accumulated capital.
10. Have an independent accountant review the final annual bridge.

## Publication rule

No NRF figure enters the final executive summary unless its accounting basis, date and currency are explicit.
