Companion evidence register for Part V of the flagship publication.  
Evidence cut-off: 27 September 2026.  
Status: partially populated. Permit existence, permit conditions and observed compliance are recorded separately.

## Control rule

Each project is tracked through six different evidence classes:

1. application and public notice;
2. environmental impact assessment and management plans;
3. environmental permit;
4. production licence environmental obligations;
5. monitoring, incidents and regulator enforcement;
6. spill response, insurance and financial assurance.

A project is not described as environmentally compliant merely because a permit exists.

## Liza Phase 1 permit chronology

The official permit record states:

- application submitted 5 July 2016;
- approved EIA dated 1 June 2017;
- Environmental and Socioeconomic Management Plan dated 1 June 2017;
- revised Oil Spill Response Plan and Wildlife Response Plan dated 1 June 2017;
- environmental permit issued 1 June 2017;
- permit modified 12 May 2021. [S48]

This sequence gives the flagship a dated example of environmental assessment occurring after the 2015 commercial discovery but before production began.

The 2021 EPA notice says the permit modification responded to resumed flaring and projected exceedance of the EIA's estimated flaring volume. The agency said the modification introduced emissions reporting, technical flaring requirements, time limits and payment of US$30 per tonne of CO2e for flaring beyond the specified timelines. [S55]

The publication must still test observed performance. The next dataset needs:
- monthly flaring volume;
- duration and cause;
- permitted versus exceptional flaring;
- tonnes CO2e;
- payments assessed;
- payments received;
- equipment failures;
- corrective action;
- inspection findings.

## Uaru permit controls

The official Uaru environmental permit states an effective period from 27 April 2023 through 26 April 2028. The permit text gives the EPA authority to order sampling and analysis of project discharges, effluent or waste by an approved independent laboratory at the permit holder's expense. It also reserves the authority to suspend, modify or cancel the permit under specified conditions and links annual review to the previous year's required audit. [S50]

Those provisions establish regulatory powers. They do not establish the frequency with which powers have been exercised or the findings of any audit.

The Uaru production licence separately requires compliance with environmental law, adequate expert personnel and equipment for spill response, dispersant arrangements and access to capping-stack capability. [S56]

The final flagship should map every response obligation to evidence of:
- equipment physically in-country;
- subscription contracts;
- mobilization time;
- drills;
- regulator observation;
- stock inspection;
- personnel competence;
- transboundary notification;
- replacement after use or expiry.

## Financial assurance register

Financial assurance must be decomposed into separate legal instruments:

- operator primary liability;
- commercial insurance;
- parent/company guarantee;
- affiliate guarantee;
- capping and containment service subscriptions;
- pollution-response contracts;
- government contingency resources;
- compensation and claims procedure.

Required fields:
- instrument name;
- parties;
- effective date;
- maximum amount if any;
- scope of covered loss;
- exclusions;
- attachment point;
- currency;
- claims jurisdiction;
- proof of financial capacity;
- termination rights;
- regulator access;
- public disclosure status.

A headline "coverage" number will not be presented unless these terms are independently verified.

## Spill response stress tests

The flagship will test institutional preparedness under at least four scenarios:

### Scenario A: controlled surface release
Test local containment, boom availability, skimming, temporary storage, shoreline protection and waste disposal.

### Scenario B: subsea loss of well control
Test capping stack mobilization, intervention capability, relief-well assumptions, dispersant logistics, aerial surveillance and sustained response.

### Scenario C: adverse weather plus spill
Test port closure, aviation limits, responder safety, communications redundancy and delayed external support.

### Scenario D: transboundary movement
Test notification, evidence-sharing, neighbouring-state coordination, compensation, fisheries closures and jurisdiction.

These are preparedness tests, not predictions of event probability.

## Evidence hierarchy for environmental claims

Highest weight:
- signed permits;
- enacted/commenced law;
- regulator inspections;
- laboratory results;
- audited monitoring reports;
- authenticated judgments;
- independently verified incident records.

Supporting evidence:
- EIA models;
- operator monitoring;
- government statements;
- academic studies;
- civil-society submissions;
- media investigations.

Where sources conflict, the final chapter will state the disagreement and identify what evidence would resolve it.

## Publication boundary

The environmental chapter will neither assume environmental success from permit issuance nor infer regulatory failure from the existence of incidents. It will test the full chain from assessment through permit, monitoring, enforcement, remediation and financial responsibility.
