# TECHNICAL ANNEX K
## Government implementation templates and readiness instruments

Purpose: provide copy-ready institutional templates for a prospective petroleum-producing country. These templates are neutral administrative tools and require adaptation to national law.

## K1. Exploration licence decision sheet

Fields:
- application ID;
- applicant;
- beneficial owners;
- acreage;
- work programme;
- financial capability;
- technical capability;
- environmental screening;
- conflicts;
- fiscal terms;
- data obligations;
- relinquishment;
- security;
- decision;
- reasons;
- publication date;
- appeal.

The decision file should preserve rejected alternatives.

## K2. Adviser conflict declaration

Every external petroleum adviser should disclose:
- current operator clients;
- former operator employment;
- financial interests;
- family interests;
- competing government engagements;
- confidentiality obligations.

The procuring agency records mitigation or exclusion.

## K3. Commercial discovery notice

The state record should identify:
- well;
- test;
- resource classification;
- operator declaration;
- regulator review;
- independent technical review;
- next appraisal step.

A discovery notice should not include unverified public-revenue estimates.

## K7. Spill-readiness certificate

The regulator verifies:
- incident command;
- capping access;
- vessels;
- aircraft;
- booms/skimmers;
- dispersant policy;
- lab;
- wildlife;
- claims;
- transboundary contacts;
- exercise date;
- corrective actions.

The certificate records evidence, not a generic "ready" statement.

Report:

- financial fund;
- debt;

- human capital indicators;

## K23. Cabinet decision memo

Every material petroleum policy memo should show:
- issue;
- evidence;
- legal authority;
- options;
- fiscal effect;
- environmental effect;
- social effect;
- risks;
- implementation;
- review.

The document supports accountability while preserving lawful cabinet confidentiality where applicable.

## K24. Annual institutional review

Ask:
- What rule failed?
- What data were missing?
- Which forecast was wrong?
- Which audit is late?
- Which permit needs revision?
- Which project lacks maintenance?
- Which community grievance remains open?

Publish a non-confidential action plan.

## K25. Twenty-four-month pre-first-oil programme

Months 1-3: legal map, data inventory, environmental baseline.
Months 4-6: fiscal benchmark, regulator plan, emergency map.
Months 7-9: revenue-fund options, audit protocol, ownership register.
Months 10-12: draft laws, metering protocol, spill drill.
Months 13-15: contract/fiscal publication rules, audit team, review panel.
Months 16-18: synthetic revenue transactions, parliamentary test, regional response.
Months 19-21: combined shocks and corrections.
Months 22-24: independent readiness review.

## K26. Five-year production programme

Year 1: reconcile cash.
Year 2: verify domestic value.
Year 3: verify project outcomes.
Year 4: test resilience.
Year 5: independent institutional review.

## K27. Annex conclusion

Templates convert principles into records. A country is better prepared when each decision leaves an auditable file, each transaction reconciles and each public claim can be tested.
