## Definition of done per volume

1. 120–160 independently inspectable substantive pages, with no repeated narrative or blank-page padding.
2. Primary-source register, page-specific evidence log and explicit distinction among evidence, assumptions, and the author's reasoning.
3. Documented international comparisons, counterexamples and qualitative or quantitative stress tests appropriate to the topic.
4. Auditable code/data and sensitivity analysis for numerical claims, or documented empirical limitations when defensible estimates are unavailable.
5. Skeptical-review record, corrected substantive errors, unresolved objections and honest disclosure of whether outside specialists reviewed the work.
6. Searchable PDF, bookmarks, table of contents, working citations, accessible figures, publication metadata, accurate page count, SHA-256 hash and QA report.
7. Standalone leadership brief, complete Markdown and PDF assets in a GitHub PR, plus combined publication archive after all ten pass QA.
8. No premature website publication or externally peer-reviewed claim.
