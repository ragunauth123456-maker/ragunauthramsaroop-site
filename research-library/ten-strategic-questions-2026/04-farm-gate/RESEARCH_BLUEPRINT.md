# The New Economics of the Farm Gate

**Independent ten-volume research series, September 2026. Prepared for Ragunauth Ramsaroop.**

Status: phase-one dossier prepared (17-page PDF and editable manuscript in the author delivery archive). This GitHub folder currently contains the research blueprint, not that PDF or full manuscript. The target 135-page flagship edition remains under development and requires additional empirical evidence and independent technical review.

## Original research thesis
Agricultural technology earns adoption through verified risk-adjusted farm income, local advice, accessible financing, market access and reliable operating infrastructure. Yield alone is not a welfare measure.

## Proposed analytical framework
FIELD: Farm economics, Information, Experimentation, Local access, Durable value. This is an original research-design label, not a published or externally validated standard.

## Ten research chapters
1. Farm-household balance sheets and liquidity
2. Field experiments and causal comparison
3. Biologicals and context-specific agronomy
4. Digital advisory channels and accessibility
5. Financing, input prices and effective costs
6. Storage, cold chain and off-take markets
7. Locally validated AI advice
8. Data rights and farmer consent
9. Diverse farm systems across geographies
10. Scalable income and food-system resilience

## Quantitative and adversarial workstreams
- Fictional per-hectare income and rainfall stress
- Input-credit and commodity-price double shock
- Platform outage and unequal farmer access

## Evidence limitations
Global farm surveys have distinct geographies, crop distributions and sampling frames and do not justify extrapolation to all farmers.
