# The Accountable Investor Communication System

**Independent ten-volume research series, September 2026. Prepared for Ragunauth Ramsaroop.**

Status: phase-one dossier prepared (17-page PDF and editable manuscript in the author delivery archive). This GitHub folder currently contains the research blueprint, not that PDF or full manuscript. The target 125-page flagship edition remains under development and requires additional empirical evidence and independent technical review.

## Original research thesis
AI-mediated investor communications require enforceable information rights, source provenance, supervisory accountability, record retention and independent failure recovery. Automation volume alone is not evidence of value or compliance.

## Proposed analytical framework
TRACE: Triage, Retrieve, Attribute, Check, Escalate. This is an original research-design label, not a published or externally validated standard.

## Ten research chapters
1. Institutional communications as a control system
2. Information rights and investor segmentation
3. Approved-source provenance and versioning
4. Triage, escalation and legal review thresholds
5. Record retention and audit exports
6. Confidentiality, injection and vendor risk
7. Correct-query economics and reviewer capacity
8. Error correction, adverse events and redress
9. Cross-border contractual and legal boundaries
10. Board assurance, audit and phased release

## Evidence limitations
SEC and FINRA obligations apply to their regulated entities and jurisdictions, not every investor communication worldwide.
