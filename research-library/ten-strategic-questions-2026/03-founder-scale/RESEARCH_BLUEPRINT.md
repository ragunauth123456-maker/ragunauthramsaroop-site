# Beyond the Founder’s Formula

**Independent ten-volume research series, September 2026. Prepared for Ragunauth Ramsaroop.**

Status: phase-one dossier prepared (17-page PDF and editable manuscript in the author delivery archive). This GitHub folder currently contains the research blueprint, not that PDF or full manuscript. The target 125-page flagship edition remains under development and requires additional empirical evidence and independent technical review.

## Original research thesis
At scale, founder-dependent judgment must coexist with institutional governance, credible financial controls, succession readiness and distributed product experimentation. A $10 billion valuation is a sampling convention, not a causal threshold.

## Proposed analytical framework
SCALE: Succession, Controls, Architecture, Leadership, Experimentation. This is an original research-design label, not a published or externally validated standard.

## Ten research chapters
1. Valuation, revenue and company-scale definitions
2. Founder bottlenecks and delegated decisions
3. Board independence and minority rights
4. Business-unit architecture and shared platforms
5. Cash conversion and acquisition discipline
6. Leadership bench and emergency succession
7. Acquisition integration and synergy verification
8. Operating cultures across jurisdictions
9. Experiments, risk limits and learning
10. Ownership structures and long-term continuity

## Evidence limitations
Public success stories have strong selection and survivorship bias. Private valuations across funding rounds are not directly comparable.
