# Insuring Wealth in a Transforming Asia

**Independent ten-volume research series, September 2026. Prepared for Ragunauth Ramsaroop.**

Status: phase-one dossier prepared (17-page PDF and editable manuscript in the author delivery archive). This GitHub folder contains the research blueprint, not that PDF or full manuscript. The target 125-page flagship edition remains subject to further empirical work and independent technical review.

## Original thesis
Asian high-net-worth insurance expansion requires suitability, family liquidity and succession analysis, insurer asset-liability discipline, risk distribution and jurisdiction-specific consumer protections.

## Proposed analytical framework
WEALTH: Wealth lifecycle, Exposure, Advice, Liquidity, Trust, Hedging. This is an original research-design label, not a published, independently validated industry standard.

## Ten research chapters
1. Country-specific HNWI and financial-asset definitions
2. Family and owner-business balance sheets
3. Product suitability and surrender liquidity
4. Distribution incentives and adviser conduct
5. Succession and beneficiary governance
6. Long-duration asset-liability matching
7. AI-supported service and advice boundaries
8. Currency, capital-mobility and geopolitical shock
9. Health, catastrophe and longevity exposure
10. Persistency, policyholder value and capital resilience

## Model and adversarial workstreams
- Fictional asset-liability capital buffer under three shocks
- Currency restrictions and correlated surrender stress
- AI suitability errors and beneficiary-dispute case

## Critical inference boundary
Private HNWI estimates and 2030 wealth forecasts are source-specific projections, not realized future facts. Product legality, taxes and suitability must be localized.
