# The Global Balance Sheet 2026

**Independent ten-volume research series, September 2026. Prepared by Ragunauth Ramsaroop.**

Status: phase-one dossier prepared (17-page PDF and editable manuscript in the author delivery archive). This GitHub folder contains the research blueprint, not that PDF or full manuscript. The target 135-page flagship edition remains subject to further empirical work and independent technical review.

## Original thesis
Asset and debt resilience depend on maturity, currency, liquidity, distribution and interconnected sector positions rather than a single aggregate leverage headline. Public options must be assessed for transparent trade-offs and distributional effects.

## Proposed analytical framework
STOCK: Sectoral positions, Tenor, Outside exposures, Cash flows, Knock-on effects. This is an original research-design label, not a published, independently validated industry standard.

## Ten research chapters
1. Stock-flow consistency and sector definitions
2. Sovereign maturity and debt-service pressure
3. Households, real assets and liquid reserves
4. Banks, funds and leverage amplification
5. Corporations and private credit refinancing
6. Cross-border currency and funding mismatch
7. Equity concentration and AI capital exposure
8. Energy, shipping and trade-price shocks
9. Low-income and small-state exposures
10. Institutional shock response, distribution and data gaps

## Model and adversarial workstreams
- Illustrative interest-service/revenue sensitivity
- Linked sovereign, bank and private-credit shock
- Country-specific import and currency scenarios without election predictions

## Critical inference boundary
Every 2026 growth figure must be identified as a dated forecast or realized estimate with its population. Hypothetical financial contagion is not a prediction of which economy or political actor will succeed.
