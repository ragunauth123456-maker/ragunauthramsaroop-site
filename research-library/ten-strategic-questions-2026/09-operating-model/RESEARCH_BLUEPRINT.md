# Why Operating Model Transformations Fail

**Independent ten-volume research series, September 2026. Prepared by Ragunauth Ramsaroop.**

Status: phase-one dossier prepared (17-page PDF and editable manuscript in the author delivery archive). This GitHub folder contains the research blueprint, not that PDF or full manuscript. The target 125-page flagship edition remains subject to further empirical work and independent technical review.

## Original thesis
Changing an organization chart produces limited value unless actual decision rights, cross-functional interfaces, operational capability, information flow and counterfactual measures change with it.

## Proposed analytical framework
ALIGN: Aim, Leadership, Interfaces, Governance, Normalization. This is an original research-design label, not a published, independently validated industry standard.

## Critical inference boundary
There is no universally established six-pitfall taxonomy that explains every operating-model failure, and pre/post changes alone do not prove causal benefits.
