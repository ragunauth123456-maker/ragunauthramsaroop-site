## Shared publication and assurance requirements

1. Register exact source, publication date, population, geography, denominator, methodology and page references. Distinguish reported observations from institutional interpretation and author inference.
2. Seek actual operating data or administrative series and obtain consent for any named institutional case. In their absence, maintain a visible data-gap register.
3. Include failure and counterexample cases, not solely positive marketing examples. Separate fictional adversarial exercises from documented events.
4. Reproduce each quantitative claim from declared source tables and versioned code, then stress correlation, omitted variables and adverse distributions.
5. Review legal, privacy, financial, operational and worker-rights implications in the relevant jurisdiction. The international comparisons must not transplant one country's rules into another.
6. Preserve material critical review and disputed findings. Independent external review is a separate prerequisite to claiming that work has been independently validated.
7. Publish finished PDF, Markdown source, dataset/code where permitted, exact licensing, accessible charts, SHA-256 release manifest and tested website links as one release.
