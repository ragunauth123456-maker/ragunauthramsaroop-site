# Commercial Intelligence in Real Time

**Independent ten-volume research series, September 2026. Prepared for Ragunauth Ramsaroop.**

Status: phase-one dossier prepared (17-page PDF and editable manuscript in the author delivery archive). This GitHub folder contains the research blueprint, not that PDF or full manuscript. The target 125-page flagship edition remains subject to further empirical work and independent technical review.

## Original thesis
Real-time AI-assisted selling has material value only when incremental margin survives credible randomized or quasi-experimental testing and privacy, fairness and service-quality controls.

## Proposed analytical framework
SCOPE: Signals, Causal tests, Offers, Protection, Economics. This is an original research-design label, not a published, independently validated industry standard.

## Ten research chapters
1. Signal provenance and endogenous customer data
2. Incrementality versus attribution
3. Pricing decisions and contribution margin
4. Personalization, privacy and consumer rights
5. Qualified leads and sales capacity
6. Forecast drift and market shocks
7. Customer-service recovery and complaint rights
8. Local consumer law and governance
9. Experiment portfolio design
10. Capital cost, scale and vendor portability

## Model and adversarial workstreams
- Hypothetical incrementality and contribution-margin model
- Price fairness and customer-data misuse case
- Experiment-contamination and competitor-response scenario

## Critical inference boundary
Forecast accuracy is not causal incremental profit. The FTC staff's U.S. market findings cannot be treated as globally binding law.
