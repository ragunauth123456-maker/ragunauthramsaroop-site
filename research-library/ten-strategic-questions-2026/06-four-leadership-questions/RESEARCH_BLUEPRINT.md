# Four Questions Defining Leadership in the AI Era

**Independent ten-volume research series, September 2026. Prepared for Ragunauth Ramsaroop.**

Status: phase-one dossier prepared (17-page PDF and editable manuscript in the author delivery archive). This GitHub folder contains the research blueprint, not that PDF or full manuscript. The target 135-page flagship edition remains subject to further empirical work and independent technical review.

## Original thesis
Enterprise AI strategy must jointly answer where audited value arises, who owns each consequential outcome, which signals would reverse the plan, and how quickly workers and institutions can absorb technological change.

## Proposed analytical framework
VALUE / OWNER / SIGNAL / ABSORB. This is an original research-design label, not a published, independently validated industry standard.

## Ten research chapters
1. Define value beyond adoption figures
2. Assign ownership of AI consequences
3. Assess genuine worker absorption capacity
4. Select decision-relevant signals
5. Map trade, chip, electricity and vendor dependencies
6. Preserve human challenge skills
7. Public and private deployment differences
8. Govern data rights and information integrity
9. Use staged investments and exit criteria
10. Measure adaptive leadership and institutional learning

## Model and adversarial workstreams
- Hypothetical adoption and correctly verified output model
- Simultaneous service, skills and trade shock
- Independent redress and supplier-termination stress tests

## Critical inference boundary
Potential occupational exposure differs from realized job displacement, and AI-generated output volume differs from net economic value.
