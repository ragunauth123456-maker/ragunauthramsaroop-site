# Physical AI and the Next Frontier of Work

**Independent ten-volume research series, September 2026. Prepared for Ragunauth Ramsaroop.**

Status: phase-one dossier prepared (17-page PDF and editable manuscript in the author delivery archive). This GitHub folder currently contains the research blueprint, not that PDF or full manuscript. The target 135-page flagship edition remains under development and requires additional empirical evidence and independent technical review.

## Original research thesis
Embodied AI should be assessed as a safety-critical operating system. Robot demonstrations are not evidence of reliable performance on real industrial terrain or proof of net labor displacement.

## Proposed analytical framework
SAFE: Site qualification, Assurance, Failure recovery, Economics. This is an original research-design label, not a published or externally validated standard.

## Ten research chapters
1. Physical AI versus legacy robotics definitions
2. Long-run reliability and distribution shift
3. Functional safety and autonomous handoff
4. Whole-life unit economics
5. Harsh and remote operating environments
6. Synthetic simulation and real-world validation
7. Workforce participation and changing tasks
8. Cyber-physical security and updates
9. Procurement claims and trial design
10. Industrial transitions and distribution of gains

## Quantitative and adversarial workstreams
- Fictional reliable task throughput
- Network-loss and fleet-update scenarios
- Safety-rated intervention and whole-life cost tests

## Evidence limitations
Industrial robot installations in IFR statistics are not a count of fully autonomous humanoids. GenAI task exposure cannot forecast physical robot employment effects.
