# From Strategy to Mobilization

**Independent ten-volume research series, September 2026. Prepared by Ragunauth Ramsaroop.**

Status: phase-one dossier prepared (17-page PDF and editable manuscript in the author delivery archive). This GitHub folder currently contains the research blueprint, not that PDF or full manuscript. The target 125-page flagship edition remains under development and requires additional empirical evidence and independent technical review.

## Original research thesis
Execution is a system of decisions, scarce capacity, accountable dependencies, measurement and correction rather than a presentation timeline.

## Proposed analytical framework
MOVE: Mandate, Ownership, Velocity, Evidence. This is an original research-design label, not a published or externally validated standard.

## Ten research chapters
1. Where strategy becomes operational
2. Mandate and decision rights
3. People, suppliers and permits as critical-path constraints
4. Defining benefits with appropriate counterfactuals
5. Portfolio sequencing under shared capacity
6. Incentives, milestones and measurement gaming
7. Operating cadence and blocker removal
8. Stakeholder participation and challenge
9. Managing complexity and unexpected change
10. Independent value verification

## Quantitative and adversarial workstreams
- Fictional multi-resource bottleneck model
- Milestone-completion versus post-adoption outcome test
- Combined leadership turnover and funding shock

## Evidence limitations
Project-management survey associations do not establish a universal causal impact of one management method.
