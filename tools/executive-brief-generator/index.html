Executive decisions

How to use this free Executive Brief Generator

Enter the decision required, context, verified facts, unknowns, stakeholders, risks and proposed next action. The tool assembles a concise one-page decision structure for review.

Who this helps

Managers, senior executives, students and nonprofit leaders.

Example application

Turn an evolving regulatory issue or difficult project escalation into a consistent management brief without uploading sensitive facts..

Limits and interpretation

The output is a practical starting point, not a verified professional assessment. Confirm the facts, jurisdiction, assumptions and intended purpose before using any result for a consequential decision.

Frequently asked questions

Does the generator make recommendations automatically?

No. It structures your inputs so decision-makers can separate facts, assumptions and proposed actions.

Can I use this for board papers?

It is a starting structure. Add options analysis, assurance, legal review and the governance requirements of your organisation.

How do I export?

Use Copy result or Print / Save PDF in your browser.
