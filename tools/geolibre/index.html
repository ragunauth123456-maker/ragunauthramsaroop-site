What you can investigate

Mining and responsible land use

Explore public elevation, drainage and land-cover datasets. Combine verified concession or protected-area boundaries where permitted.

Water and climate resilience

Use terrain and hydrology processing for initial watershed, flood-exposure and surface-water studies. Validate results with qualified specialists.

Infrastructure and eco-tourism

Visualise public roads, settlements, satellite layers and candidate routes. Check mapping dates, positional accuracy and data rights.
