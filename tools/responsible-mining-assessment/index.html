Mining and sustainability

How to use this free Responsible Mining Self-Assessment

Review sixteen practices across environmental stewardship, safety, rights, local content, community relationships, closure, governance and reporting.

Who this helps

Mining operators, contractors, students and sustainability managers.

Example application

Use a preliminary assessment to identify missing evidence and decide which issues require a qualified independent audit..

Limits and interpretation

The output is a practical starting point, not a verified professional assessment. Confirm the facts, jurisdiction, assumptions and intended purpose before using any result for a consequential decision.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a responsible-mining certification?

No. It is an educational self-assessment, not an accredited assurance scheme.

Can a contractor use it?

Yes. Adjust applicability to its actual scope rather than assuming mine-owner responsibilities.

Does a score prove compliance?

No. Evidence, applicable rules and independent review determine formal compliance.
