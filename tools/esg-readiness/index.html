ESG and governance

How to use this free ESG Readiness Diagnostic

Score twelve governance, environmental and social practices from 1 to 5, based on evidence rather than ambition. The tool averages the responses and shows the lowest-rated dimensions as priorities.

Who this helps

Sustainability teams, company directors, small organisations and students.

Example application

A mining supplier preparing its first sustainability report can identify where evidence, accountability or stakeholder engagement needs attention before selecting reporting standards..

Limits and interpretation

The output is a practical starting point, not a verified professional assessment. Confirm the facts, jurisdiction, assumptions and intended purpose before using any result for a consequential decision.

Frequently asked questions

Does a high score mean my company is ESG compliant?

No. This is a self-assessment, not a compliance audit, certification or investor rating. Requirements vary by jurisdiction and sector.

Where is the assessment saved?

The answers remain in the browser and are not uploaded by this tool. Use Copy or Print to retain the result.

What should I do with the results?

Validate the lowest-rated areas with documented evidence, assign an accountable owner and agree measurable actions for the next 90 days.
