Privacy and free tools

Most tools on this site calculate and prepare results directly in your browser. Entered text and numerical inputs are not uploaded to this website by these tools. If you print, copy or share a result, your browser, device or chosen destination handles that action.

Analytics

Google Search Console provides aggregated organic-search performance for this site. An optional Cloudflare Web Analytics integration is prepared but is not active until a site-specific beacon token is added. No tracking cookies or account creation are required by RR Free Tools.

External links

Official agency and research links lead to third-party websites with their own privacy terms. Avoid entering sensitive personal information on shared devices.

Scope

These tools are educational and practical planning aids, not professional certification, engineering design or jurisdiction-specific legal advice.
