Privacy and free tools

Most tools on this site calculate and prepare results directly in your browser. Entered text and numerical inputs are not uploaded to this website by the free tools. If you print, copy or share a result, your browser, device or chosen destination handles that action.

Website analytics

This website uses Google Analytics 4, measurement ID G-28FHYG7T0G, to understand visits, page use and technical performance. Google Analytics Consent Mode is configured so analytics storage is denied by default. Analytics cookies are enabled only when a visitor chooses Allow analytics. Advertising storage, advertising user data and ad personalisation remain disabled. With analytics storage denied, Google Analytics Consent Mode can still send limited cookieless measurement signals rather than analytics cookies.

The site records privacy-safe interaction names such as tool_started, tool_completed, result_copied, result_printed, tool_shared, template_downloaded, white_paper_opened, topic_opened and profile_opened, report_downloaded and feedback_opened. These events include page or tool identifiers only. The site does not send the text, résumé content, grievance data, stakeholder names, calculator values or other form inputs entered into the free tools as analytics parameters.

Google Search Console separately provides aggregated information about how pages appear in Google Search, including impressions, clicks and search queries. Search Console data does not represent every website visit.

Changing your analytics choice

Your preference is stored locally in your browser under rr_analytics_consent_v1. Clearing site storage resets the choice. On a new browser or device, the preference will be requested again.

External links

Official agency and research links lead to third-party websites with their own privacy terms. Avoid entering sensitive personal information on shared devices.

Scope

These tools are educational and practical planning aids, not professional certification, engineering design or jurisdiction-specific legal advice.
