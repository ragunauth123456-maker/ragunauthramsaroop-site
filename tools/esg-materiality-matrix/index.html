How to use this tool

Organise ESG issues before a more rigorous materiality or double-materiality process involving stakeholder and financial evidence.

Who this helps

ESG teams, boards, students and smaller organisations.

Responsible use

The result is a screening aid, not formal legal, engineering, financial, environmental or assurance advice. Validate the relevant facts, standards and jurisdiction before consequential decisions.
