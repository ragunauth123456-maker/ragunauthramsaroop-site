How to use this free Free Executive Templates Library

Start from blank, editable decision briefs, stakeholder engagement plans, ESG action plans and operational registers. Adapt each template to the organisation’s own controls and document requirements.

Who this helps

Managers, community teams, students, NGOs and directors.

Example application

Download a grievance register or decision memo instead of starting a new document from an empty page..

Limits and interpretation

The output is a practical starting point, not a verified professional assessment. Confirm the facts, jurisdiction, assumptions and intended purpose before using any result for a consequential decision.

Frequently asked questions

Do the downloads require an email address?

No. The files are free and directly downloadable.

Are the templates legal or regulatory forms?

No. They are generic management aids and must be reviewed against your organisation’s requirements.

Can I edit and reuse them?

Yes. The plain-text and CSV formats are designed for adaptation.
