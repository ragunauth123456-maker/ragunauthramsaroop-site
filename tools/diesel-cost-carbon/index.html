Mining and climate

Mining Diesel Cost & CO₂ Calculator

Estimate fuel cost, operational diesel emissions, production intensity and the effect of a user-selected diesel-reduction scenario.

FreeNo accountBrowser-sideMining & Operations

How to use this tool

Use current fuel consumption and delivered fuel-price assumptions to screen cost and carbon exposure before a formal financial or GHG inventory review.

Who this helps

Mine operators, finance teams, sustainability practitioners and students.

Responsible use

The result is a screening aid, not formal legal, engineering, financial, environmental or assurance advice. Validate the relevant facts, standards and jurisdiction before consequential decisions.
