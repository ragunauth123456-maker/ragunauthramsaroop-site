Institutional risk

How to use this free Government Relations Risk Mapper

Rate eight risk exposures, including regulatory uncertainty, approval dependencies, government stakeholder alignment and execution. The tool reports a screening average and the largest exposures.

Who this helps

Corporate-affairs leaders, project managers and compliance teams.

Example application

Before an infrastructure permit discussion, structure the evidence required, decision authority, stakeholders, escalation route and commitments already made..

Limits and interpretation

The output is a practical starting point, not a verified professional assessment. Confirm the facts, jurisdiction, assumptions and intended purpose before using any result for a consequential decision.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a regulatory compliance opinion?

No. Obtain country-specific advice and verify permit obligations with the relevant institutions.

Should a risk score determine an engagement strategy?

No. Validate the scoring against facts, issue severity, legal authority and affected stakeholders.

Can I map more than one project?

Run separate assessments and compare the documented underlying assumptions.
