Local economic development

How to use this free Local Content Value Calculator

Enter total procurement, procurement with qualifying local suppliers, a target share, supplier count, workforce count and the local-worker count. The tool estimates shares and the extra local procurement needed to meet the scenario target.

Who this helps

Procurement teams, suppliers, project sponsors, students and community planners.

Example application

Compare annual local procurement performance with a transparent internal target without inventing indirect economic multipliers..

Limits and interpretation

The output is a practical starting point, not a verified professional assessment. Confirm the facts, jurisdiction, assumptions and intended purpose before using any result for a consequential decision.

Frequently asked questions

Is the local spend definition legally certified?

No. Use the applicable jurisdiction’s current local-content definition and procurement verification rules.

Does this estimate indirect jobs or economic multipliers?

No. It reports only the direct input-based shares and shortfall, without unsupported multiplier estimates.

Is the target a legal requirement?

No. The target is user-entered and is not presented as a statutory threshold.
