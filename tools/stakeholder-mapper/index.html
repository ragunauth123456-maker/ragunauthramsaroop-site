Stakeholders and social performance

How to use this free Stakeholder Mapping Tool

Add stakeholders and assign influence, interest and relationship strength from 1 to 5. The tool orders a screening engagement list and suggests manage closely, keep satisfied, keep informed or monitor.

Who this helps

Community teams, NGOs, project managers and public-affairs professionals.

Example application

A project team can map regulators, community groups, employees, suppliers and neighbouring land users before developing a documented engagement plan..

Limits and interpretation

The output is a practical starting point, not a verified professional assessment. Confirm the facts, jurisdiction, assumptions and intended purpose before using any result for a consequential decision.

Frequently asked questions

Does influence equal stakeholder legitimacy?

No. Influence is only one planning dimension. Consider rights, vulnerability, impact, legitimacy and representation independently.

Can I include sensitive names?

Avoid entering confidential or sensitive personal information on shared devices. Inputs are processed within the page.

Is the priority order final?

No. Validate the ranking through engagement and impact assessment.
