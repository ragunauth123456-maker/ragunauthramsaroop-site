How to use this free Water Demand & Storage Calculator

Estimate daily and annual water needs from people, per-person use and optional industrial use. Account for a planning loss factor, storage buffer and a user-selected demand-surge scenario.

Who this helps

Schools, households, community planners, site managers and students.

Example application

A remote facility can compare the storage volume implied by seven versus fourteen days of entered demand without assuming that one fixed benchmark suits every climate or project..

Limits and interpretation

The output is a practical starting point, not a verified professional assessment. Confirm the facts, jurisdiction, assumptions and intended purpose before using any result for a consequential decision.

Frequently asked questions

Is the result a potable-water engineering design?

No. Water quality, treatment, firefighting, drought duration, rainfall and regulatory requirements require qualified design.

Can I change consumption assumptions?

Yes. All inputs, including per-person water use and loss percentage, are adjustable.

Does the tool save my household or project details?

No. The inputs remain in the browser.
