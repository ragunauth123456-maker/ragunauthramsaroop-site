The Physical Limits of Artificial Intelligence

Energy, water, critical minerals and the infrastructure requirements of advanced AI and hypothetical superintelligence, 2026-2035.

Ragunauth Ramsaroop · Georgetown, Guyana · Research cut-off: 25 September 2026 · 25-page research edition.

Executive summary

AI growth depends on physical infrastructure. The research examines electricity, grid connections, cooling, water, chips, minerals, finance, cybersecurity and public acceptance as one connected industrial system.

Decision standard

The paper separates observations, institutional projections, author calculations and hypothetical scenarios. Future superintelligence is treated as a contingent scenario rather than an established capability or forecast.
