Research monograph & strategic white paper · 20 September 2026

Water Security as Economic Security

Water as a first-order variable linking food, power, industry, cities, finance, trade and national resilience.

Research mandate

The work integrates hydrology, public health, agriculture, energy, manufacturing, data-centre, trade and financial-risk evidence. It separates scarcity from service failure, withdrawals from consumption, and physical risk from governance risk.

Analytical framework

The research develops a twelve-domain Water Security Systems Framework, a Water Security Resilience Index concept and a Strategic Water Exposure Score for structured analysis of systems, assets and sectors.

Strategic value

The central proposition places water inside national economic strategy, corporate capital allocation and infrastructure planning rather than treating water as a narrow environmental topic.

Evidence boundary

The work is independent research with an evidence cut-off of 20 September 2026. Its frameworks are analytical tools and require local calibration before operational use.
