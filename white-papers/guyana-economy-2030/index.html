Academic & executive research monograph · September 2026

Guyana Economy 2030

Converting exceptional resource wealth into durable national prosperity.

Research mandate

The study examines the economic architecture Guyana might possess by 2030, the conditions required to convert petroleum income into durable national wealth, and risks capable of weakening the transition.

Evidence discipline

The analysis combines 2026 macroeconomic evidence, public finance, petroleum development, sector data, international research on resource-rich economies and original scenario frameworks. Observed data, institutional projections and author scenarios are separated throughout. The research cut-off is 19 September 2026.

National wealth conversion

The framework tests whether resource income strengthens financial, physical, human, productive, institutional, natural, social and spatial capital. The focus is the quality of conversion rather than headline growth alone.

Decision use

The monograph is independent analytical work. It is not an official forecast, fiscal audit or investment prospectus. Scenarios are used to test conditions and policy choices rather than predict a guaranteed 2030 outcome.
