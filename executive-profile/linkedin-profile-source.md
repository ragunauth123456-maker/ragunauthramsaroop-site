## About

I work at the intersection of business, government, communities and operations across mining and natural resources.

I currently serve as Liaison Director within the Social Responsibility Department of AGM Inc., part of Zijin Mining Group. My remit spans social responsibility, ESG-facing priorities, government and regulatory engagement, stakeholder strategy, community relationships, executive communication and cross-functional coordination.

My career has developed across regulated banking, commercial strategy and large-scale gold mining. Since joining AGM Inc. in 2020, my scope has expanded across government and regulatory relations, corporate coordination, compliance, aviation, stakeholder engagement and social responsibility. The common thread is translating external expectations into clear internal action, while keeping commitments connected to evidence, ownership and follow-through.

My leadership interests include Corporate Affairs, Government Relations, External Affairs, ESG, Sustainability, Social Performance, Strategic Programs, Responsible Business and stakeholder strategy. I bring operating context across mine sites, contractors, logistics, permitting, local content, community engagement, renewable-energy positioning and institutional relations.

I hold an MBA, completed Zijin Mining Group's First Non-Chinese Employee Training at headquarters in 2023, was selected for the Management Reserve Program in 2023, and received the Best of Best Second Merit Award in 2021. I am also featured by the Climate Leadership Research Centre at the University of Oxford.

I am open to senior country, regional and global mandates where business performance, government expectations, regulation, communities and long-term institutional trust need to move together.
