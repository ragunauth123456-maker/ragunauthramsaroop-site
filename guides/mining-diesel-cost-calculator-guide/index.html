Mining Diesel Cost & Carbon Calculator Guide

How to screen mining diesel cost, carbon exposure and a reduction case using editable inputs.

How to screen mining diesel cost, carbon exposure and a reduction case using editable inputs. The useful output is not a single number or ranking. The useful output is a transparent record of assumptions, evidence gaps and the next validation step.

Use the tool as a screen. Keep the inputs editable, preserve source dates and move material decisions to the appropriate professional, regulatory or organisational review.

Recommended sequence

  1. Reconcile annual diesel volume to operational records.
  2. Enter the delivered fuel price and the emission factor used for the chosen reporting boundary.
  3. Test a reduction percentage as a scenario rather than a forecast.
  4. Compare the result with operational feasibility, equipment duty cycles and implementation cost.

What to record before sharing a result

Record the reporting period, source of each material input, the definition used for key terms, the methodology version and the limitations that remain unresolved. A result without this context is easier to misread.
