How to calculate direct local procurement and workforce shares with a transparent definition.

How to calculate direct local procurement and workforce shares with a transparent definition. The useful output is not a single number or ranking. The useful output is a transparent record of assumptions, evidence gaps and the next validation step.

Use the tool as a screen. Keep the inputs editable, preserve source dates and move material decisions to the appropriate professional, regulatory or organisational review.

Recommended sequence

  1. Define what qualifies as local under the applicable rule or policy.
  2. Use verified spend and workforce records for the same reporting period.
  3. Calculate direct shares before discussing broader economic effects.
  4. Record the target as a scenario unless a binding requirement has been independently confirmed.

What to record before sharing a result

Record the reporting period, source of each material input, the definition used for key terms, the methodology version and the limitations that remain unresolved. A result without this context is easier to misread.
