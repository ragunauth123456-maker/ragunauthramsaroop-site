Responsible Procurement Self-Assessment Guide

How to review procurement integrity, supplier expectations and evidence gaps without treating a self-assessment as certification.

How to review procurement integrity, supplier expectations and evidence gaps without treating a self-assessment as certification. The useful output is not a single number or ranking. The useful output is a transparent record of assumptions, evidence gaps and the next validation step.

Use the tool as a screen. Keep the inputs editable, preserve source dates and move material decisions to the appropriate professional, regulatory or organisational review.

Recommended sequence

  1. Review conflicts, integrity screening and tender controls.
  2. Check environmental, safety and human-rights expectations in supplier requirements.
  3. Review local supplier development separately from supplier qualification.
  4. Retain an audit trail for approvals, contracts, performance and corrective actions.

What to record before sharing a result

Record the reporting period, source of each material input, the definition used for key terms, the methodology version and the limitations that remain unresolved. A result without this context is easier to misread.
