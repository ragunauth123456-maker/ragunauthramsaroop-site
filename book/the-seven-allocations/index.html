ALLOCATIONSAttention · Decisions · Capital · Talent · Trust · Time · Optionality

A chief executive does not run every function. The chief executive decides where the enterprise places its scarce resources, which truths receive attention, which choices become commitments, which people hold the hardest roles, which relationships receive trust, which hours receive protection, and which future paths stay open.

The framework

Seven scarce resources shape enterprise direction.

The book treats CEO work as an allocation system. Each allocation affects the others, so leadership quality depends on the portfolio rather than one isolated decision.

01Allocation

Attention

Protect the signals and questions that deserve executive focus.

02Allocation

Decisions

Build decision rights, speed and quality without centralising every choice.

03Allocation

Capital

Make strategy visible through where money and capacity move.

04Allocation

Talent

Place strong people on the work with the greatest enterprise value.

05Allocation

Trust

Treat credibility and stakeholder permission as operating capacity.

06Allocation

Time

Protect the calendar and run immediate and long-horizon clocks together.

07Allocation

Optionality

Build choices before shocks, constraints or opportunities make them urgent.
