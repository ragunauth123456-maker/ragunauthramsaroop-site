Externally visible examples

Two public examples with independent media context.

These examples demonstrate public-facing professional context. They are deliberately narrower than a traditional marketing case study because the source evidence does not establish sole project ownership.

01Media verified

Renewable-energy expansion at Aurora

Sunday Stabroek · 7 December 2025

Public reporting on AGM Inc.'s renewable-energy expansion quotes Ramsaroop on the programme and its sustainability model.

What the evidence demonstrates

  • Translation of a major operating investment into stakeholder context

This evidence supports public-facing ESG and sustainability communication. It does not claim engineering, design, procurement or technical ownership of the solar programme.

Interpretation boundary

Evidence should narrow a claim, not inflate it.

Where the source demonstrates public communication, the site claims public communication. Where the professional record is owner-ratified, it remains labelled owner-ratified. Project ownership, technical authorship and quantitative outcomes are not inferred unless the evidence supports them.
