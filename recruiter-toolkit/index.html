Shortlist discipline

What to verify before progressing.

A strong search process should test fit and evidence rather than simply repeat the résumé.

Role scope matches the actual organisational challenge, not only the title.
Government, regulatory, ESG and stakeholder responsibilities are relevant to the mandate.
Project 2126 and the Evidence Vault support the consequential claims being considered.
International mobility, work authorisation and compensation are confirmed separately for the specific market and role.
Interview judgement

Four questions that test judgement, not résumé recall.

The Decision Brief establishes fit. These questions are better used in the interview to test how the experience translates into decision-making.

  1. How would you diagnose a government or stakeholder issue before engaging externally?
  2. How do you keep ESG commitments connected to operational reality?
  3. How do you build institutional relationships that survive changes in people and leadership?
  4. Where have cross-functional gaps created the greatest execution risk, and how would you close them?
