How value should be measured

Beyond activity. Toward stronger organisational capability.

These are measurement dimensions for a serious mandate, not historical performance scores. A company should establish its own baseline before setting targets.

01Value domain

Institutional trust

Preparedness · commitments · closure

Are important institutional issues visible early, owned clearly and followed through?

02Value domain

ESG execution

Evidence · ownership · operating alignment

Can sustainability claims be traced to accountable action and real practice?

03Value domain

Stakeholder confidence

Signals · concerns · follow-through

Are expectations and recurring concerns reaching decision-makers before they become crises?

04Value domain

Issue readiness

Facts · escalation · response discipline

Can leadership respond consistently when a sensitive issue emerges?

05Value domain

Cross-functional alignment

Handoffs · owners · decision clarity

Do external commitments and internal operating responsibilities stay connected?

06Value domain

Executive communication

Context · decision · recommendation

Can leadership see the facts, implications and next action without digging through correspondence?
