Free-Use Statement

These public tools and templates are intended to help people think, learn and work more effectively without a paywall.

What you are welcome to do

  • Use the free tools for lawful personal, educational and organisational work.
  • Download, edit and adapt the free templates for your own legitimate use.
  • Share links to the tools, research and Resource Centre.
  • Reference the methodology pages when explaining how a result was produced.

Please do not

  • Present a screening result as formal certification, legal advice, engineering approval or independent assurance.
  • Imply that Ragunauth Ramsaroop, Aurora Gold Mines Inc. or any institution endorses your project or result.
  • Resell the unmodified template pack as a standalone paid product.
  • Remove material warnings or alter methodology descriptions in a way that makes a result misleading.

Attribution is appreciated when these resources materially support published work. This page is a plain-language use statement, not a substitute for legal advice or a formal open-source software licence.
